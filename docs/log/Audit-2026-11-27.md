## Not one language (part 3)
The card says Cavalry, Siege, Stop, Hold, Retreat, Shelter, Prep; the code says Riders, Engines, mpause/mute, stand, homing/recall, alarm/emg, board. Player text is British but for "Town Center" and "Civilization". 45% of names in index.html are one letter; `W`, `MW` and `WD` mean different things in different files. menu.js averages 273 characters a line. About 266 KB of the 592 KB of loaded books is notes for people.
