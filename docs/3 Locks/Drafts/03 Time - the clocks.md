# Draft 3: Time, the clocks [2026-11-19 11:38]

STATUS: OPEN TALK (Albert, Nov 19 11:25-11:36). Nothing ruled. Came out of trees: no real rate (felling, digging, regrowth) means anything without a time rule.

## The problem
- Three clocks today: walking at real pace (1x); the day 240 s (360x); the season 360 s, the year 1,440 s, 6 days a year (about 21,900x).
- One linear squeeze cannot hold all three: with the day's squeeze a season is 21,900 s (6 h 5 min), a year 24 h 20 min.

## His ideas
- Proportional: the season by the day's own ratio (11:29-11:33; his note: 1,440 / 240 = 6, x 0.25 = 360). Result: 21,900 s; or 360 s if "proportional" means a quarter of the game's own year.
- "There needs to be a formula" (11:34). An exponent (11:34).

## Formulas tried
- Power law on every span, game = real^0.482: fails, four seasons do not add up to a year.
- Power law on cycles only (day, year), season = year / 4: alpha = ln 240 / ln 86,400 = 0.482; year 4,129 s (69 min), season 1,032 s (17 min), 4.3 days a season. Works; a chosen mapping, no source.
- Design formula: season = match length / seasons wanted.

## Claude's proposal (11:38), not ruled
- The fix is units, not a squeeze. The clash came from fixing 1 wood = 1 kg. Free the unit and today's rate (D88, 0.75 wood/s) is real on a chosen clock:
  - day clock: 180 wood a game day = a woodcutter's 2.8 t day, so 1 wood = about 15.5 kg; a 100-wood tree = one timber tree of about 1.6 m3.
  - year clock: 1,080 wood a game year = about 1,000 t a worker-year, so 1 wood = about 1 t = about 1 m3 green wood; a 100-wood tree stands for a grove.
- Precedent (from memory, NOT opened): map symbols drawn larger than scale, the legend carries the truth; Kriegsspiel 1824 and miniature wargames keep separate figure, ground and time scales; "representative days" in energy models (a year shown by a few sample days).
- Walking stays at figure scale (what the eye sees), the one declared exception.
- Rough: assumes chopping without trips, every day of the year.
