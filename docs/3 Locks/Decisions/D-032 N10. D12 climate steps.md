N10. D12 climate steps. DECIDED (Albert, Oct 31), replacing my first suggestion.
 Cold and wet steps are layers of thermal resistance: heat loss Q = dT / R_total, series layers add, R_total = R_body + sum(R_i), so the slowdown scales with 1 / (1 + sum(a_i)), each a_i being the layer's resistance as a multiple of what the soldier already wears. Wool cloaks a = 1 gives exactly half, a second layer gives a third, never zero. The fx data already adds; only the formula that reads it changes. Unit: 1 clo = 0.155 m2 K/W (ISO 9920, ASHRAE 55; from memory, not looked up). Weather glass is a barometer, not insulation: its cold and wet slowdown cut has no physical basis; drop it and give it a forecast or lead-time effect, or a small abstraction (to settle at build; Albert said yes to all). Ice steps (Horseshoes, Crampons) are friction, not insulation: the ice code already uses a friction coefficient and slip chance (Hanson et al. 1999, 37-events about line 680); check at build how they enter muOf.
