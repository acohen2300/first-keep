N6. D6 crowns. DECIDED by delegation (Albert, Oct 31): use best accepted practice across the top games of the genre.
 Finding: there is no single standard. Two families: flow scores (resources gathered, value of enemies destroyed: Age of Empires II and StarCraft II style, from memory) and stock scores (value of what is alive divided by 5: Age of Empires 4, read from a forum write-up of the author's testing). Age of Empires I (read from the openage reverse-engineering notes): 0.5 point a kill, 1 per building destroyed, economy from gold gathered and traded. Chosen for the crowns: Wealth = everything gathered, counted once (stores no longer added on top); War = cost value of enemy people and buildings destroyed, animals excluded; Wellbeing = people x (1 - sick share) + care buildings + laurels, with health taken from the existing sickness model (today p.health stays 100). Each is one measure per crown, ranked as now. Open: confirm the Age of Empires II and StarCraft II formulas from a source before building. Code home: crownsOf 39-games 143-170; PEACE.crown.
