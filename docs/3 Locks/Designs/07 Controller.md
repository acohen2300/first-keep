## The rules of a tap [2026-11-11 20:45]
1. Opening War picks every soldier (All is lit). Class, Squad and Idle change who is picked; the rings on the board show them. [2026-11-11 20:45]
2. Attack lights. A tap on the board only places the aim. Execute turns green; nothing moves until it is tapped. [2026-11-11 20:45]
3. Defend and Retreat act on the first tap. Defend goes to whoever is being attacked; Retreat goes home. [2026-11-11 20:45]
4. Tap a soldier, or drag a box round some: the Controller opens with those picked. A tap on the ground or a foe is then an order at once (tap one, tap there). [2026-11-11 20:45]
5. Closing War lets the soldiers go. [2026-11-11 20:45]

## The six meanings (agreed for the whole game; the Controller uses the first three) [2026-11-11 20:45]
Gold outline = selected. Green = ready. Dim = not available. Draining ring = time. Blue = next. Red fade = cannot afford. [2026-11-11 20:45]
