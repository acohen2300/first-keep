# Design 16: the menu law (N69)

Albert, Nov 18 2026, 08:12 to 10:31 (talk, his sketches in `docs/4 Now/Notes/`, mocks `mockups/dock/175` to `197`; every word in The List, section 30). His go, 10:31: "Ok let's do it."

## The law (09:43)
"Top stays. Gives direction. Bread crumbs. Selections. Dynamic."
- The line never changes: Home · Jobs | Build · … · W (War as a W in a circle, 09:59).
- Jobs and Build float as words with a pipe, close together, the open one underlined (09:15, 10:31). Tap one to open, tap the other to switch, tap the open one again to close (08:44). Nothing open at the start: only the line (08:40).
- The gap on the line says what to do next in italics, centred in the free span (10:31), or holds Build's group words.
- The second row is the breadcrumb, then the actions with pipes between them (10:27, 10:31). The first crumb is large, the rest the same size (10:31); every crumb is tappable to go back to it (10:31).
- One Cancel, the same everywhere: it drops the last crumb (09:59).
- Pick, then confirm: Go for a job, Build for a building. After the confirm the crumb reads "Going" / "Building" for a moment, then the menu closes (10:27).

## Jobs (08:40, 09:05, 09:08)
- Floating − + above each job tile; a + alone above People (trains a villager). Tiles: People, Food, Wood, Gold, Stone, Iron (gold before stone, 08:44).

## Build (08:40, 10:18)
- Line: Town | Work | Army | Keep | Tech (Learn renamed Tech), plain words split by thin bars, the open one gold.
- Row: Build → Town · Cancel. Tap a building: Build → Town → House · Build | Cancel.

## A picked villager (09:41, 09:56, 09:59)
- Line: "choose a task" (rows closed), "choose a job below" (Jobs), "tap Go to send" (a job picked); on Build the group words.
- Row: Villager → state · Garrison | Delete | Cancel ("you don't need stop": a tap on ground moves it, a tap on a resource sends it). With a building picked, Build joins the actions.
- Jobs: Food, Wood, Gold, Stone, Iron, no People and no − +; a job tapped lights, Go takes the first slot; tap the lit job again to drop it.
