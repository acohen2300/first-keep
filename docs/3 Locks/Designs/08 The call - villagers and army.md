# 08 The call: villagers and army (Albert, Nov 12, 2026, 15:53-16:42, yes yes)

The yardstick for P2 (the job door). Anything in the code that disagrees with this page is to be replaced or deleted. Built: nothing yet.

## 1. The call (both)
- Tap a unit (working or not): it is ready to do what you say (the call), and its card opens. Not "selected": "ready".
- Tap 2 is the task. After the task the unit is let go (the call ends).
- Tap the same unit again: hang up (let go, no task; it carries on with what it was doing).
- Cancel on the card: same as hang up.
- Tap another unit without New Group: the call moves to that unit.
- New Group on the card, then tap others: each joins. A group is temporary and splits after its task unless it has a next order.
- Two doors, one idea: a tap starts with a person; the Controller starts with a group. Both hand the task to the same order function (one home).

## 2. Villager tap 2
| Tap 2 on | Villager |
|---|---|
| Empty ground | Go there and wait, ready to do something soon |
| A resource (berry, tree, boulder, gold, fish) | Gather it |
| An animal | Hunt it |
| A construction site | Help build |
| Your own building, damaged | Repair |
| Garrison on the card | Garrison |
| Build on the card | The Build menu: pick, place, she goes |
| An enemy or a wolf | Attack |
| A spot that is full | Toast "Full: see closest alt"; she stays ready |
- After a build: if it is a resource building she gathers there; if more construction is queued she goes to it; otherwise, if she was doing something before, she goes back to it; if not, she stays idle.
- Villagers attack only if attacked first, or commanded.

## 3. Soldier tap 2
| Tap 2 on | Soldier |
|---|---|
| Empty ground | Go there |
| An enemy unit or building | Attack |
| A wild animal | Attack |
| Your own building with room | Garrison |
| A construction site or a resource | Toast "Can't"; he stays ready |
- A squad (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie) is set and stays together after a task; a group is temporary.

## 4. Attention (on the card)
- Normal: today's behaviour, minus Focus on every tap (E1027 goes).
- Focus: does not leave the job unless reasonably required. The task ends: go to the Town Center and wait (a soldier: back to his post, else the Town Center). Attacked: fight.
- Mobile: first responder. First picked for +, a call for a villager or soldier, a site needing a builder, Defend. (Albert: fine as is; revisit later.)

## 5. Load (villager setting)
- Drop: drop off what she carries, then the new task.
- Destroy: throw it down and go straight to the new task (AoE2's villagers lose their load on a switch of resource).

## 6. The card (both)
1. Status: idle / gathering: gold / building: home / hunting: deer / repairing: stable (soldier: marching to, fighting: spearman, garrisoned in).
2. Group: what it is doing and how many others do the same ("with 7 others gathering"), with Select the rest. Soldiers: squad shown too.
3. Orders: first X, then Y, done ✔. Add task runs the call again with prompts on the card ("Tap her next task") until Done.
4. Attention: Focus / Mobile / Normal.
5. Build (villager) / Formation (soldier).
6. Cancel.
7. History: time spans, what and how much (1:00-2:22 gold gathered 784; 2:23-2:26 walked to food; 2:27-5:31 gathered 2,020 food; 5:32-now farming).
8. Statistics: spawned at; total gathered; total consumed (soldier: kills, damage dealt and taken, battles).
9. Exit: delete the unit (it dies and disappears). Two taps: Exit, then "Tap again".
