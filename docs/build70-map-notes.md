## What could not be done cleanly, or needs a decision

1. **The citadel is still half a square off the middle.** Its ring of walls centres on (1005, 1005), so each side's nearest gates stand 75 (north, west) or 85 (south, east) from the Throne. The walk to the Throne itself is the same for all four (the gate only sits at a different point on the causeway). Making the ring exact needs two-square gates, a change to the citadel; not made without Albert's yes.
2. **The Town Center's plan counts fish without a dock** (`turtlePlan`). With lakes now nearer some homes than others, the card is optimistic for a corner with a lake just outside its clearing (2.2 villager-minutes to Bronze against 3.9). The turtle measure was to be kept, so it is flagged, not changed.
3. **Fewer rivers on average: 2.3 a map, against 4.** Water now keeps out of the whole clear radius (360; the old rivers came within 236 of a Town Center), and a river must be long enough for its two fords. The bounds were set by a scratch simulation of the same rules: 4 to 9 lakes of 50 to 160 give about nine tenths of the old water and fish, with a river on 58 of 60 maps.
4. **The hazards now differ between harness games, and the fingerprint's reason leaves them out.** `tests/balance.py` and the gate's fingerprint start fresh pages, where nothing is ticked, so each seed draws its own hazards (the fingerprint's seed 7: Hardest, Famine, Bad weather and Plague, level 9). The fingerprint was accepted with the reason as given ("one seed, one map; lakes and rivers random; the Throne central; the fairness re-roll cut"); "hazards drawn by lot when none are picked" also moved it, and belongs in the reason at the final accept. If the harness should keep measuring like with like across builds, it could pin its hazards (`F.SET.hz`); left as it is, since the harness measures the game as shipped.
5. **A carried game ("New land", your age kept) with hazards drawn by lot has no line naming them.** The verb's own line ("New land...") stands; the Score card shows the level.
6. **Pre-existing, not changed:** a "far berries" group (six bushes) walled in by forest on about one seed in five, before and after (16, 17, 19, 29... before; 9, 17, 18, 21... after): the forest's reach check does not see the clearance erosion of Build 63. And `laySpots` keeps finds off a point called "this corner's shrine on its shore", though the one shrine stands in the Throne.
