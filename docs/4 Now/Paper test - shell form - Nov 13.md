## What it shows
1. The form holds: no question was unanswerable for any of the four world things.
2. The code-only answers are the same four sections every time: Place, how a life ends, Governed by, States. Those are the laws to pull into one home, in that order of size: States, Governed by, Place.
3. The lake is the furthest out: no identity at all.
4. More is already in place than the audit said: the tap rule is a table in the Screen book (SN43) and the card's rows are keyed by ticks (SN4). Correction to the audit: the tap rule is not homeless; what is missing is that every tap goes through it.
5. Colour is in the art, not on the shell; buildings and finds have no mass.
