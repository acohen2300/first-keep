# Rivals on the phone (desk read, Nov 13; not played)

- War Legends (Gear Games, July 2026): fantasy, 2 alliances, 6 races, heroes and scrolls; gold and wood only; campaign, ranked PvP to 3v3, 5-minute arena; online only; power sold outside the match.
- Art of War 3 (Gear Games): modern war, 2 factions, land, sea and air; long campaigns; clan world war; online only; users fault its monetization and stability.
- Northgard (paid PC port): 6 clans, 4 ways to win (conquest, fame, lore, trade), winter as a planned threat, building slots, 11-chapter campaign, 6-player multiplayer.
- First Keep ahead: five resources and a market; offline, one file, nothing sold for power; free building with ages and civs.
- First Keep behind: no multiplayer; no campaign or tutorial; one way to win that reads clearly; polish; no sea or air fighting.
- Cheapest lesson: Northgard's legible victory paths and winter. First Keep has the wonder and the weather; they need to be made readable.
Sources: gamedeveloper.com (War Legends launch), skich.app, apps.apple.com (Art of War 3, Northgard), pcgamer.com (Northgard review), pcgamesn.com.
