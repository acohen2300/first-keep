# The Q&A format: decisions one at a time [2026-11-01 07:45]

Albert cannot work through a long document of questions. Decisions go in chat, in order, one at a time: [2026-11-01 07:45]

- **Problem:** what is wrong or open, in a line or two. [2026-11-01 07:45]
- **Idea:** the proposal, with its real-world basis if there is one, in a line or two. [2026-11-01 07:45]

About three lines in all, no prose. Wait for his answer. Record it as you go, with the time, his words in bold, and what follows from it. Then the next one. [2026-11-01 07:45]

When all are answered, lock each decision as a file in `docs/3 Locks/Decisions/` (his exact words, never reworded). [2026-11-01 07:45]
