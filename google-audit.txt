Detailed Audit by Question Area
1. Logic Holes & Edge Cases
 * Pathfinding Global who Leak (index.html:936–937, 969, 987, 999, 1032, 1072, E328):
   * walkable(k) relies on the module-global who to test friendly gates (!fire('shut', GATES.get(k), who)) and Levees flood immunity (stat('', who.side, 'flood')). However, who is only set inside walkTo() and reach(). Every call to wayLen() (L987), vacant() (L999), shove() (L1032), slide() (L1072), sealPath() (econ.js:74), and routeK() (combat.js:146) runs with who === null, treating every side's own gates as shut walls and Levees-protected flood tiles as impassable.
 * A Binary Heap Corruption (index.html:954–964, E90)*:
   * In findPath(), when a shorter path g is found to a tile q already in open, line 963 mutates fs.set(q, g + hx(q)) in-place and calls push(q) a second time without sifting the existing entry, corrupting the binary heap ordering.
 * Catastrophic 93,636-Tile Flood-Fill in reach(side) (index.html:970, E453):
   * reach(side) stamps a 60-unit circle around the Town Center and, for every walkable tile k not yet in set, calls findPath(k, (q) => void set.add(q), k, Infinity). Because the goal callback always returns undefined, findPath exhaustively flood-fills the entire connected board (~150 ms per unreached tile in the stamp).
 * Guarded Finds & Interior Bushes Walled In by Forest (index.html:217–244, E47–E49, E652):
   * openWays(fixed) runs at L227 before FN.lay (den, drifters at L231) and camp (L232) are placed, and only checks the 4 immediate cardinal neighbors (step.map(d => t.k + d)), which are all blocked (OCC === 1) for the center bush of a 3×3 berry patch. On Seed 1, robber camp k = 73922 and bushes k = 69899, 84942, 85247 have zero reachable spots.
   * Additionally, index.html:230 scales every 'tree' not in fixed by LD.amt.wood, unintentionally scaling every forest tree across Z1–Z4 instead of only Z3 finds (E654).
 * Line-of-Sight Footprint & Unfinished Site Bugs (index.html:1278–1282, E148–E150):
   * tops() (L1278) includes 0%-built construction sites (t.done < 1) at their full finished building height, and look() (L1282) only rebuilds tops() when topN !== things.length (missing same-tick swaps where one thing dies and one is placed).
   * sees(e, t) (L1279) starts ray-marching at s = e.n || 1 from the building's center (cellOf), skipping e.n / 2 tiles outside a building's walls (3 tiles outside a 6×6 Town Center).
 * Upkeep Free-Food Bypass on Farms & Walking Gatherers (econ.js:170–173, E349):
   * fromFind(u) is true while a villager is merely walking across the map toward a food find (u.then === 'gather'), and when targeting a Farm (t.kind === 'bld'), KIND[u.target.kind] is undefined, so farm workers neither add to need nor deduct from u.target.amount—eating 100% free forever.
 * Hall Queue Timer Doubles First Item (menu.js:115–116, hallRead):
   * Wait: in hallRead() (L116), i ? (at += ECON.timeOf(h.side, c)) : at is right for hallRead, whereas in ROWS.queue (L224), i ? ECON.timeOf(t.side, c) : t.qt shows individual build times instead of cumulative wait times (inconsistent with hallRead).
 * Cost Discounts & Camp Surcharges Ignored in VERBS.train (menu.js:166, E186):
   * VERBS.train.label and VERBS.train.whyNot read raw D.cost[c] instead of ECON.priceOf(ME, c, b), ignoring civilization/tech cost discounts (O154 feudal, O224 corvee) and the 1.25× War Camp training surcharge (tentCost).
 * School Graduation Keeps Old Sprite (econ.js:290, graduate, E460):
   * graduate(u) updates u.code = teachTo(u) and u.hpMax, but never updates u.look = FK.SH[u.code].look (copied onto u at spawn in index.html:1042). A Spearman (UM1) taught at the School into a Swordsman (UM6) keeps drawing as a Spearman forever.
 * Pay Ransom / Prisoner Conversion Loses Unit When TC Ring Is Full (econ.js:294–296, 302, E461):
   * takePrisoner calls WAR.die(v) (leaving a corpse and blood scar for a living captive, and triggering vassal levy death penalties). When ransomed (payRansom), gold is deducted and the prisoner is spliced before home1(side, x.code) attempts spawnBy(FK.seatOf(side), c)—if the Town Center's perimeter is full, the ransomed unit is permanently lost.
 * Muster Trains Stone-Age Units in Late Ages & Drops Overflow Units (combat.js:43, econ.js:88, E386, E402):
   * lack(i) (combat.js:43) uses FK.kinds(cls).find((c) => FK.aged(...)), which always picks the first (lowest-age) unit of each class (Spearman, Archer, Chariot, Ram) even in the Gunpowder Age.
   * mustered(b, job) (econ.js:88) spawns all mustered units in one loop around the 3×3 War Council (BP10); any units past the ~16 perimeter spots fail spawnBy (null) and are silently lost after being paid for at 2× cost, and FK.on('spawn') runs rebattalion() before u.bn = job.bn is assigned.
 * Caltrops Disarmed After 1st Hit (combat.js:344–346, 349, E519–E520):
   * spring() sets b.seen[v.side] = true on the 1st hit (L346), and traps() (L344) skips any trap where b.seen[v.side] is true—making D280's hits: 3, again: 2 on caltrops unreachable against the same side. Also die(v) at L349 omits b, losing War crown and vassal credit for trap kills.
 * Hill Milestone Prints 100% Slowdown (menu.js:384, 386, 404, E645, E648):
   * FK.footing(u) returns .grade (not .hill). When MS_TEST.hill triggers, lessonTick() calls slowed('hill') (FK.footing(u)['hill'] > ML.hill -> false), passing u = undefined to msFill(), which formats Math.round(100 - null * 100) = 100% ("Steep ground slows this column by 100%").
 * F39 Guide & History Issues (menu.js:425–453, books/words.js:11, Rule X73):
   * BS1 (Throne, look: 'throne') has no entry in WD.why (J7), so guide().buildings has why: undefined for Throne (violating X73).
   * UN = Object.keys(U).filter((c) => D.cost[c]) (menu.js:429) excludes all 11 unpriced/wild units (MH1–MH7, MW3–MW8), orphaning WD.why.wayfarer (J7 also has orphaned keys venture, satrapy, sixth).
   * homeOf(c) (menu.js:426) uses .find(), so BTC6 (Training tent) always gets trains: [] in guide().buildings, and US5 (Envoy, priced at 40g in D.cost but not in D.trains) falls back to at: "the Town Center" while missing from BTC1's trains list.
2. States That Can Stick
 * AI Soldiers Permanently Stuck in 'hold' After chokeClaim, allyDuty (sendHelp), or Drop-Off Raids (combat.js:132, ai.js:33–34, 137, 143, 156, econ.js:442):
   * When a march finishes, brain() (combat.js:132) sets u.job = 'hold' and leaves u.goal set to the destination tile. After u.drill.until expires on a choke (chokeClaim) or after sendHelp finishes assisting an ally, nothing resets u.job to 'idle' or clears u.goal. Because decideWar (ai.js:137) filters by !u.goal, and chokeClaim (ai.js:33) and loot (combat.js:329) filter by u.job === 'idle', those AI soldiers stand in 'hold' forever and can never join another attack wave, choke, or loot party.
 * AI loot() Detachment Permanently Stuck at Drifters' Camp (combat.js:302, 326–330, E582, E588):
   * guardsLeft(t) (combat.js:302) checks v.find === t && !SIDES[v.side] && alive(v) without checking FK.has(v, 'Fight'). Peaceful Drifters (MH4, atk: 0) have v.find === t, so guardsLeft(f) is always true. If an AI marches a detachment to MG9/MG10 with < 200 gold (or < 240 gold for MG10, since L326 hardcodes L.gold = 200 while Mercenaries cost 240 in D305!), the soldiers never attack the peaceful drifters, !guardsLeft(f) is never true, and half the AI's army stays stuck at the camp for the rest of the game.
 * Un-Squaded Player Soldiers Stuck Un-Sendable (u.sent = true) (combat.js:150, 161, 171, E412, E413):
   * Ordering soldiers without a battalion (u.bn == null, e.g. before researching command) to open ground sets u.sent = true (L161). When they arrive (O.arrived = true), u.sent is never cleared, so idleSoldier (!(u.sent && u.bn == null)) returns false forever, and tapping Attack says "None idle: all are guarding."
 * Garrisoned Soldiers Trapped Inside Buildings After Work (allClear) (econ.js:254–259, combat.js:239, E444):
   * alarm('hands') garrisons soldiers (u.inside = b, u.job = 'inside'), but emg only records villagers and fright() only runs on villagers. Clicking Work (ECON.allClear()) releases villagers and leaves every garrisoned soldier trapped inside u.inside forever.
 * Unit Card Follow Immediately Cancels Itself (menu.js:163, 324, index.html:1335, E608):
   * Opening a unit's card via double-tap (menu.js:324) calls FK.select(null) before setting S.subject = t. Clicking Follow on that card sets FK.cam.follow = true, which followCam() (index.html:1335: if (!FK.picked().length) cam.follow = false) cancels on the very next frame.
 * Ghost Drag & Edge-Scroll (drag, edge) Stick (menu.js:289–292, 362):
   * In menu.js:289, if (!g) return; executes before if (ev === 'pointerup' || ev === 'pointercancel') { drag = edge = null; }. If S.ghost clears while a finger is down near the screen edge, edge and drag stay non-null and cause runaway camera panning the next time any building ghost is opened.
 * Villager Fleeing While Returning to Drop-Off (u.then === 'drop') Loops Into Danger (econ.js:232–233, index.html:777):
   * leave() (econ.js:233) nulls u.target = null when a drop-off building becomes unsafe, but only calls lose(u) if u.job === 'gather' || u.then === 'gather'. A villager with u.then === 'drop' finishes walking to the unsafe drop-off, enters u.job === 'drop' with u.target === null, and FK.dropFor() (index.html:777, which does not filter out ECON.unsafe) sends it right back into the same unsafe drop-off.
 * Shared t.noPath Poisons Finds Across All 4 Sides (econ.js:127, 130, E222):
   * When one side's villager cannot path to a find t (e.g. walled off in another corner), t.noPath = bell is set directly on t, hiding that resource from all other sides for 60 seconds.
3. Anything a Tap or Hold Can Do Twice (or Swallow)
 * Long-Press (data-h) Re-Render Eats click and Swallows the Next Tap (menu.js:263–271, E428):
   * Holding a [data-h] button for 320 ms sets S.held = true and runs act() -> render(true), which replaces #panel/#base .innerHTML while the finger is still down. Because the button DOM node is destroyed before pointerup (or if the finger slides off before release), no click event fires to clear S.held = false. Furthermore, pointerdown only resets S.held = false on ui (L267), not on topEl or qaEl, so the next tap on Mute or Quick-Attack (qaEl) is swallowed by if (S.held) { S.held = false; return; }.
   * Also, pointermove (L271) checks single-frame Math.hypot(e.movementX, e.movementY) > D.touch.slop (26 px in a single event!) instead of distance from pointerdown coordinates (lpX, lpY), so normal finger dragging never cancels the hold timer.
 * Double-Tapping Empty Ground Swallows Every Second Move Order (menu.js:323–324, E429):
   * On empty ground, t is null. Tapping twice within 320 ms and 26 px evaluates L.key === t (null === null -> true), setting dbl = true and returning early at if (dbl) { if (t) { ... } return true; } without issuing the move order.
 * Multi-Squad WAR.go(aim) Stacks All Squads on the Exact Same Tiles & Emits Triple Messages (combat.js:179, E416):
   * When 2 or 3 squads are ready, WAR.go(aim) calls ready.forEach((i) => send(i, aim)). Each send() dresses its squad onto the exact same formation slots at aim.k and fires a separate "X marching" message.
4. What a Save & Reload Mid-Game Breaks
 * Shared Reference Breakage (index.html:351–360): As noted in Critical Bug #3, pack()/unpack() splits shared references to WAR.ORD, b.camp, and u.amb, immediately killing active missions ("Mission lost"), deleting order lines, breaking group ambushes, and multiplying campsOut by 5–8×.
 * Empty SEEN on First Ticks After Reload Terminates Active Combat Missions (index.html:369–371, combat.js:166, 173):
   * load(R) does not call look(EYE.tick) after restoring entities. For up to 0.5 s (EYE.tick) after reload, SEEN is empty, so FK.inSight(v) (when called without eye in foesNear at combat.js:166 and bestCounter at ai.js:63) returns false for all enemies. If ordersTick() runs during that window while soldiers are fighting (!walk.length), F.length is 0 (L173), prematurely ending active combat missions as 'done'.
 * Shallow Mutation of BASE0.things in unpack() (index.html:357, E658):
   * const fresh = BASE0.things; followed by Object.assign(fresh[e[TG.base]], e) mutates BASE0.things in-place! Subsequent saves/loads in the same session diff against a corrupted BASE0.things.
 * PRNG play Resets to Seed Start on Every Reload (index.html:1040, 1520):
   * dice(seed) does not expose or save its internal 32-bit state a, so loading a save resets the play (rng()) sequence to t = 0.
 * fightT Save-Delay Blocks Saves on Cross-Map Marches, Hunts, and New Games (index.html:347, 362–363, E659):
   * fight checks u.target && u.target.hp > null && u.target.side !== ME && u.target.side != null, which is true while marching across the map toward an enemy building (WAR.order sets u.target immediately) or while hunting wild animals (side === -1). Worse, fightT is never reset in start(), blocking saves at the start of a new game if the previous game ended with fightT > K.t.
5. What Drifts Over a 60-Minute Game
 * Ruts (RUTS) Wiped Out in 20s Instead of 600s by Math.floor (index.html:1151, E552 vs D294 / QP):
   * Every 10s (RU.heal), RUTS.forEach((n, i) => { RUTS[i] = Math.floor(n * HEAL); }) floors a Float32Array where a villager pass adds ~2.2 walker-seconds (HEAL ≈ 0.9835). Math.floor strips at least 1.0 every 10s, erasing ruts in 20–30 seconds instead of decaying with the 600s time constant.
 * Ventures Drive s.gathered Negative, Breaking Economy Score & Vassal Tribute (econ.js:26, 318, 526, E268, E482):
   * earn(side, 'gold', shareUp(side, back), 'venture') (L318) passes why = 'venture', which earn() (L26: if (why === 'gather' || why === 'trade') s.gathered += n;) does not add to s.gathered, yet L318 subtracts s.gathered -= v.amt;! Every completed venture reduces s.gathered by 200, corrupting Economy score (econ.js:108) and allowing a vassal to keep s.gathered - s.base <= 0 (L526) and dodge tribute.
 * Un-Refunded spentBy Drifts Economy Rate Breakdown (econ.js:29, 36, E269, E271):
   * payBack() (L29) decrements s.spent1[y] and s.ledger.out[why] on queue/site cancellations, but never decrements s.spentBy[y][why], inflating orateBy in the Economy ledger breakdown.
 * Stale Path-Distance Cache t.way on Finds (econ.js:118, E307):
   * Once t.way = { b, d } is cached on a find t, it is never invalidated when walls/gates are built or destroyed or when forest trees are felled, skewing forage() decisions over a 60-minute game.
 * Permanent Per-Tick Math.exp on u.tire (combat.js:116, E408):
   * After a soldier charges once, u.tire relaxes toward 1 (D.bases.pace) via FK.relax(u.tire, 1, 10, dt). Because u.tire asymptotically approaches 1 (which is truthy!), if (u.tire) remains true forever and runs Math.exp every frame for the rest of the game.
 * Unbounded Memory Growth in ARGS (menu.js:186–187), gifts/demands (econ.js:487, 511), and Sprite Cache (SPR) FIFO Eviction (index.html:1083–1099, 1330):
   * ARGS stores entity references keyed by unit + code + currentTileK + side 4 times/sec and is never cleared (not even on start()).
   * SPR (index.html:1089) concatenates numeric keys without delimiters (lv + kit + fr) and evicts in FIFO order rather than LRU, destroying the player's Town Center and Villager sprites once SPR.size > 400.
 * Repeat Plague Caravans Reset plagueDead (econ.js:344, 476, E488, E596):
   * Each caravan returning during a plague calls outbreak(side, HE.cart, true), which resets SIDES[side].plagueDead = null (L476) and lets a single plague kill far past the 10% plagueCap (D311).
