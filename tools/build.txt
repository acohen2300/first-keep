# Build (Albert, Nov 26 16:44 "Build", after the control rule sheet talked through 14:41 to 16:42): one way to command soldiers. Pick by a tap (adds, a second tap drops), the box (0.25 s hold, soldiers only when it holds any) or the card's boxes; with soldiers picked a tap on an enemy attacks it and a tap on ground moves them; with nobody picked a tap primes a target (yellow ring) and Attack or Move on the card fire at it. Picked soldiers wear a green ring, a target under attack a red one. A tap on your own building is never a troop order. Cut: the armed Attack and Move, place-first and its dashed ring, double tap, re-picking by an old Move ring, enemy cards. Also: soldiers sent at an enemy unit seek it. Changes how every order is given. Does not change how a match with no taps plays.
