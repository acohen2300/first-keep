## Written proposals for the talk items (Oct 29, after Build 58; nothing built)
- **23 Market elasticity.** Today `mktHit` nudges a price by a hand-set step per sale and lets it drift back. The established form is constant-elasticity demand, P = P0 (Q/Q0)^(−1/ε): a price that answers quantity on a curve with one number, ε, per good (grain is inelastic, about 0.3 to 0.5; luxuries like spice elastic, 1 to 2; Deaton & Muellbauer, *Economics and Consumer Behavior*, 1980). One table row per good (P0, Q0, ε), one function, the drift replaced by Q0 recovering over time (the market absorbs what was sold). The harness's trade return (now measurable from the ledger) is the before-and-after. Small build; needs the yes.
- **44 Core direction.** What the last six builds say: the game now has three ways to be played to an end (conquest, the Throne, the Enlightenment) and three crowns that name a winner on each path. The honest gap is that the computers only walk one of them: they stall in Classical and never reach the peace path. Proposal: before any new feature, fix the computers' age stall (46) so every path is reachable by every seat; then the stranger test (51); then native (49, 50).
- **45 The one toy.** Of everything that could be the game's one signature, the Games and the hidden age are the only ones no other mobile RTS has, and they cost nothing to the war player. Proposal: make them the toy. Visible scoreboard of laurels on the Throne island, the Games drawn as a small event there (figures racing a lap), the crowns on a shareable end card. Nothing else new until that lands.
- **46 Balance.** Measured: the Fortress seat wins 7 of 12 (it never fights first and shares the vassal rule); Classical reached by 28 of 48 computer sides at 40 minutes; Medieval by none. The cause to test first: `ageReady` asks for `per.vils` villagers and `per.ageArmy` soldiers before saving for an age, and the Classical costs (600 food, 200 gold) wait on gold the computers mine late. Proposal: let the harness report minutes-to-age against gold income per seat; then move the computers' gold mining earlier in `per.build` and lower `ageArmy` for the balanced personality. One number at a time, each run against the harness.
- **47 Idle workshops.** The harness now counts "waiting for input at a workshop" (0 in the last runs since shop workers went inside; the old number came from workers standing outside). Watch it; nothing to build until it shows.
- **48 Spoilage.** Real: grain keeps a year in a dry store, meat days unsalted, fish hours. Proposal, if wanted: food stock above what a granary holds loses a share a season (an exponential decay, k per season, as the drying model); salting and the ice house (already in) cut k. It is a tax on hoarding, which the rich-and-peaceful end game may need. Not before 46.
