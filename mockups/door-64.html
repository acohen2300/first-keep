There is no music in life. Life is the music. You will notice a rhythm and a beat emerging from your kingdom: that is its own song, composed by your decisions.

Every stroke sounds when it lands, at the pace of the find it works; crews fall into step on their own; the birds, the wind and the thunder sit on top.

Work songs kept crews in time long before metronomes.
